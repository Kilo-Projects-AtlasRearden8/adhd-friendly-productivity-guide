# Introduction: What Is Low-Energy Productivity?

Most productivity books start with a question like "What do you want to
achieve today?" That is a great question if you have the energy to answer it.
On a low-energy day, it can feel like being asked to climb a mountain before
you can get out of bed.

Low-energy productivity is different. It starts with a gentler question:

**What is one tiny thing I can do right now, without hurting myself?**

## The shame problem

ADHD brains are often told they are lazy, scattered, or not trying hard enough.
That message lands as shame. Shame makes starting even harder, which creates
more shame. It is a loop, and it is not your fault.

This guide is shame-free by design:

- Resting is not failure. Rest is data.
- An unfinished to-do list is not a moral failing.
- "Doing less" can be the right move.
- You do not have to earn rest.

## How your energy works

Energy is not a single tank. Think of it as a few different batteries:

- **Mental energy** — focus, decisions, problem-solving
- **Physical energy** — movement, standing, getting through the day
- **Emotional energy** — patience, motivation, handling frustration

On a good day, all three might be full. On a hard day, one or more might be
near empty. Low-energy productivity means choosing tasks that fit the battery
you actually have, not the battery you wish you had.

## What helps, in three ideas

1. **Tiny tasks.** Make the first step so small it feels ridiculous to skip.
2. **Body-doubling.** Work near another person so your body stays on track.
3. **Energy-friendly routines.** Build routines that flex with your energy,
   instead of breaking when your energy dips.

The rest of this manual walks through each one, with worksheets you can print
and fill out by hand.

## Your first permission slip

Print this out, or copy it somewhere you can see it:

> I am allowed to do less today.
> I am allowed to rest without earning it.
> I am allowed to start with one tiny task and stop there.
> Finishing nothing is still a valid day.

You do not have to believe it yet. You just have to allow it.
