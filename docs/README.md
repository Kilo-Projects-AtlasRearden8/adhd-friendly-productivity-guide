# Low-Energy Productivity for ADHD Brains

A beginner manual for getting things done when your energy is low, your
executive function is tired, and "just try harder" has never worked.

Written by Asdale Dynamics, LLC. Plain English. Warm. Shame-free. Tested.

## Who this is for

This guide is for anyone who:

- Has ADHD (diagnosed or self-identified) and struggles to start tasks
- Runs out of energy before the day runs out of tasks
- Feels shame or guilt about unfinished to-do lists
- Wants a gentler, body-friendly way to be productive

You do not need a diagnosis to use this. You just need to be tired of
productivity advice that assumes unlimited willpower.

## What's inside

1. [Introduction](introduction.md) — what low-energy productivity means
2. [Tiny Tasks](tiny-tasks.md) — break work down until starting feels easy
3. [Body-Doubling](body-doubling.md) — work alongside another person
4. [Energy-Friendly Routines](energy-friendly-routines.md) — match tasks to the energy you have
5. [Printable Worksheets](printable-worksheets.md) — worksheets to print and fill out by hand

## The core idea

Standard productivity advice assumes you have a full battery, steady focus, and
unlimited willpower. ADHD brains often run differently. This guide starts from
what you actually have: small bursts of energy, variable focus, and a body that
needs rest.

You are not broken. The system was.

Use what helps. Ignore the rest. There is no wrong way to be productive.
