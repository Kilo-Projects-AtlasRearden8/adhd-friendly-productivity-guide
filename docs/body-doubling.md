# Body-Doubling

Body-doubling is working in the presence of another person. You do not have to
do the same task. You do not have to talk. You just share space, and the other
person's presence helps your brain stay on track.

For many ADHD brains, a body double makes starting easier, staying focused
longer, and finishing more likely.

## What body-doubling is

A body double is a person who is present while you work. They are not a coach,
a tutor, or a supervisor. They do not need to help you. They just need to be
there.

Examples:

- A friend works on their laptop at your kitchen table while you sort mail
- You and a coworker stay on a quiet video call while each of you works
- A family member reads in the same room while you fold laundry
- You go to a library or cafe where other people are working quietly

## Why it works

ADHD brains often struggle with "object permanence" for tasks — if it is not
in front of you, it can slip out of mind. Another person's presence acts like
an external reminder that the task exists. It also lowers the sense of
isolation that makes boring tasks feel unbearable.

This is not about being watched. It is about being accompanied.

## How to body-double

1. **Pick a task.** Something small and specific works best.
2. **Find a person.** A friend, family member, coworker, or online partner.
   Many ADHD communities have free body-doubling sessions.
3. **Set the terms.** How long will you work? Will you talk, or stay quiet?
   What happens if you get distracted?
4. **Work side by side.** In person or on video. The body double does their own
   thing; you do yours.
5. **Check in when you are done.** A quick "how did it go?" is enough.

## Body-doubling solo

No one available? These can help mimic the effect:

- Work in a public place where others are working (library, cafe)
- Record a short video of yourself stating what you will do, then work
- Use a focus timer with other people's voices in the background
- Tell someone when you will start and when you will finish, then report back

## Body-doubling etiquette

If you are the body double:

- Do not give advice unless asked
- Do not comment on how the other person is working
- Do your own quiet task
- Be present, not performative

If you are being doubled:

- You do not have to explain your task
- You do not have to perform productivity
- It is okay to stop and rest
