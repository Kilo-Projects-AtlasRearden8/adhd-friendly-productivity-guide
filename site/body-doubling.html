Body-doubling · Low-energy productivity

Body-Doubling

Working in the presence of another person. You do not have to do the same task. You do not have to talk. You just share space.

What body-doubling is

A body double is a person who is present while you work. They are not a coach, a tutor, or a supervisor. They do not need to help you. They just need to be there.

  • A friend works on their laptop at your kitchen table while you sort mail
  • You and a coworker stay on a quiet video call while each of you works
  • A family member reads in the same room while you fold laundry
  • You go to a library or cafe where other people are working quietly

This is not about being watched. It is about being accompanied.

Why it works

Another person's presence acts like an external reminder that the task exists, and it lowers the isolation that makes boring tasks feel unbearable.

How to body-double

  1. Pick a task. Something small and specific works best.
  2. Find a person. A friend, family member, coworker, or online partner. Many ADHD communities offer free body-doubling sessions.
  3. Set the terms. How long will you work? Will you talk, or stay quiet? What happens if you get distracted?
  4. Work side by side. In person or on video. The body double does their own thing; you do yours.
  5. Check in when you are done. A quick "how did it go?" is enough.

Body-doubling solo

No one available? Try these instead:

  • Work in a public place where others are working (library, cafe)
  • Record a short video of yourself stating what you will do, then work
  • Use a focus timer with other people's voices in the background
  • Tell someone when you will start and finish, then report back
