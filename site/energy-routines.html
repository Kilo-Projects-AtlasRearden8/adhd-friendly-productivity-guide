Energy-Friendly Routines

Most routines are written for steady energy. ADHD energy is rarely steady. Build routines that flex with your energy instead of breaking when it dips.

Map your energy

For a few days, jot down your energy level at morning, midday, and evening — low, medium, or high. You can use the Energy Map worksheet.

You will start to see patterns. Maybe your brain is sharpest after lunch. Maybe evenings are for lying down. That is useful information, not a problem to fix.

Match tasks to energy

Energy levelGood forNot good for
HighHard decisions, starting new projects, paperworkNothing — go for it
MediumEmail, dishes, tidying, short errandsBig new projects
LowRest, water, one tiny task, staring out the windowAnything requiring focus

When your energy is low, the right move is often rest — not pushing through.

Build a flexible routine

Instead of a fixed schedule, try a menu:

  • Morning (any time after you wake up): drink water, open the curtains, pick one tiny task (optional)
  • When energy is medium: one household task (10 minutes max), one message or email, a short walk
  • When energy is low: rest without guilt, one tiny task if you want, or nothing at all

The menu lets you choose what fits, instead of following a script that was written for a different brain.
